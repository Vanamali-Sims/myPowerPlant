# Usability Findings and Actions

**This is the file that closes the loop.** Running sessions is only half the method;
the evidence that testing *worked* is that the backlog changed because of it. Every
finding below traces to a decision, and every decision traces to a story, a defect,
or an explicit "won't do" with a reason.

A finding we consciously decided not to act on is a legitimate outcome — an
unexplained one is not.
